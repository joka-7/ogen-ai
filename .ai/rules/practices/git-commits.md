## Commits & PRs

- Conventional Commits: `type(scope): summary` — feat, fix, refactor, test, docs, chore, perf, build, ci. Summary in imperative mood, ≤72 chars, no trailing period.
- One logical change per commit. Don't mix refactor + behavior change in one commit.
- Body (when needed) explains *why*, not *what* — the diff shows what.
- Never commit secrets, generated artifacts that belong in build, or commented-out code.
- Breaking changes: `!` after type/scope and a `BREAKING CHANGE:` footer.
